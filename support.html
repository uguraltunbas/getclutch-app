Support

Answers to the questions people actually ask. Anything else: write to us.

01Cancel a subscription

iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Clutch › Cancel. Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

02Restore a purchase

Open the app › You › Restore purchases, signed in with the same account you bought with.

03Delete your account

Open the app › You › Delete account. It removes your profile, team, alerts, calls and Press Credits. You can also e-mail us to ask.

04Alerts don't arrive

Check iPhone Settings › Notifications › Clutch, then the app's You › Notifications. The app shows a notice when iOS has them switched off.

05Why is the record not better than picking the home team?

Because right now it isn't, and the Ledger prints what is true. Every call is timestamped before tip-off and graded after the final, wins and losses alike.

06Press Credits

Earned on the Play tab by calling games and shooting — never bought, never paid out. 100 open a 24-hour Press Pass of Pro.

07Report a bug

E-mail us with what happened, your iPhone model and iOS version. A screenshot helps.
