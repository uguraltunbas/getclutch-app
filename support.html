Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open your iPhone Settings > tap your name > Subscriptions > Clutch - AI Picks > Cancel Subscription. Subscriptions are managed through Apple, not within our app.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app > go to Profile > scroll down to Danger Zone > tap Delete Account. You can also email uguraltunbas1@gmail.com to request account deletion.

How accurate are the predictions?

Our ML model analyzes thousands of data points for each game. Predictions are for entertainment and informational purposes only. Past accuracy does not guarantee future results.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the app > go to any premium feature or the paywall screen > tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom.

Is this a gambling app?

No. Clutch does not facilitate betting, accept wagers, or process bets. All predictions and analysis are for entertainment and educational purposes only.

How do I report a bug?

Email us at uguraltunbas1@gmail.com with a description of the issue, your device model, and iOS version. Screenshots help!
